# Code of conduct

Participation should be welcoming and free from harassment, regardless of identity, background,
disability, experience or beliefs. Be considerate, discuss ideas respectfully, accept constructive
feedback and take responsibility when your actions harm someone.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, threats and sharing
another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. These standards apply in
project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

Maintainers may remove harmful material, request a correction, restrict participation or exclude a
participant. They should consider severity and repeated behavior, explain decisions where appropriate,
and protect the privacy of people reporting an incident.

A private community-reporting channel is not configured in this source snapshot. Contact the person
who supplied the source through an existing private channel to request one. Do not put sensitive
incident details in public issues. Before public launch, maintainers must publish a monitored private
contact and identify the people responsible for enforcement. Security reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This shortened adaptation draws on the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), with project-specific reporting
and enforcement text. It is not the complete Covenant template.
